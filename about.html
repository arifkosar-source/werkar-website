ABOUT WERKAR

Architectural solutions for projects in Vancouver.

Werkar brings together internationally sourced doors, windows and architectural systems with local sales, project coordination and installation planning for residential and commercial projects in Vancouver.

OUR APPROACH

A coordinated approach from product selection to installation planning.

We work with homeowners, builders, architects and businesses to assess doors, windows, glazing, aluminium systems and outdoor-living products against the building, intended use, dimensions and project scope.

  • Doors and entrance systems
  • Windows, glazing and aluminium systems
  • Pergolas and external shading
  • Product selection through installation planning
OUR PRINCIPLES

A considered approach, built on trust and accountability.
